Where is there room for more play?

A small research pilot to explore unused capacity and pricing or promotional questions, with affordability and public access in view.

Questions worth exploring

When are courses busiest, and when are player spaces unused? How do rounds and realized green-fee revenue vary? Which pricing or promotional scenarios might deserve a carefully designed test?

A useful starting point

The proposed takeaway is a concise findings brief, charts of supported patterns, documented data limitations, and a shortlist of possible next tests.

Scenarios show assumptions and uncertainty. They are not forecasts or promised revenue gains.

Keep the first step manageable

01

Agree on the question

Choose one course or one season. Confirm scope, timing and data handling before sharing records.

02

Review existing exports

Start with rounds, revenue and capacity records. Customer names, contact details and payment information are not needed.

03

Discuss what the evidence supports

Review findings and possible next tests. The course retains control of operational and pricing decisions.

Research status

A prototype, with clear limits

The golfops prototype has been explored using Dobson Ranch public data. It has not been validated for Peoria Park District. No specific revenue uplift is promised.

Time-of-day analysis requires tee-time records. Daily totals can support daily or seasonal comparisons, but cannot reveal individual tee-time availability.

The Peoria outreach brief is an invitation to discuss a pilot, not an announcement of a partnership or endorsement.
